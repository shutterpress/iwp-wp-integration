# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

Initial standalone release of FluentCart InstaWP Provisioning.

- Provision sites from paid FluentCart product feeds with snapshot, plan, expiry, and deferred setup settings.
- Track asynchronous creation, customer ownership, subscriptions, and plan changes.
- Provide customer and administrator site management plus standalone snapshot creation.
- Use a dedicated plugin identity, storage namespace, and release package.
